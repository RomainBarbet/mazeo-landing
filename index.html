Features

Everything you need to find the right match

Purpose-built for the Market Research industry. Not another generic sales tool.

Interactive Market Map

Explore the entire Market Research ecosystem visually. 1,200+ companies organized by category — End Clients, MR Agencies, Data Collection, Tech Tools, Consulting, Media, Institutionals. Click any company for deep insights.

AI-Powered Matching

Our engine scores every company pair across 17 specialization dimensions — from Brand & Communication to Healthcare & Pharma. Each match comes with a relevance score and an explanation of why it fits. The AI also recommends which contact to reach out to based on your context and objectives.

Dashboard

Manage your pipeline with status tracking, notes, and prioritized lists. Search by similarity — find companies that match the profile of your best clients or providers. Create multiple pools and export up to 20 contacts per batch.

Key Contacts

For each company, access the relevant decision-makers with their role, seniority, specialization, and direct LinkedIn profile. Reveal any prospect's professional email in one click. 5,000+ professionals already identified and classified.

AI-Powered Search & Chat

Ask Mazeo anything in natural language — "Show me MR agencies strong in healthcare with more than 50 employees" or "Which end clients are growing their insights team?". The AI queries the entire database and returns structured results instantly.

"Show me MR agencies specialized in Healthcare with 50+ employees"
"Which end clients are growing their insights team?"
"Compare Ipsos and Kantar on Brand & Communication expertise"
