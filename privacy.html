1. Who we are

mazeo ("we", "us") is operated by Romain Barbet. Our website is mazeo.co. For any question regarding your data, contact us at romain.barbet@mazeo.co.

2. Data we collect

When you request early access via our waitlist form, we collect:

  • Your email address

We do not collect any other personal data. We do not use cookies for tracking purposes.

3. Why we collect it

We use your email address solely to:

  • Send you updates about mazeo's development and launch

4. How we store it

Your email is stored securely in our database hosted on Supabase (servers located in the EU). Access is restricted to the mazeo team.

5. Third parties

We do not sell, rent, or share your email address with any third party. The only third-party service processing your data is Supabase, our database provider, which acts as a data processor on our behalf.

6. Your rights (GDPR)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Withdraw your consent at any time
  • Request data portability

To exercise any of these rights, email us at romain.barbet@mazeo.co. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Data retention

We retain your email address until you request its deletion, or until 2 years after your last interaction with mazeo, whichever comes first.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will be revised accordingly.
